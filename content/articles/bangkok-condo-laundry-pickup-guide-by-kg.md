Even worse is the **in-unit condo washing machine struggle**:
- **Humid-Season Balcony Drying**: Between May and October (and during high PM2.5 smog months from December to March), hanging damp clothes on a narrow condo balcony outside the AC condenser unit traps moisture, exhaust heat, and urban dust. ## Frequently Asked Questions About Bangkok Condo Laundry Delivery

### 1. Do I need to weigh my laundry before booking a pickup?
**No.** You never need to guess or weigh your clothes at home. Simply select an estimated weight in our [online calculator](/#book) when booking. Once your bag reaches our facility, we weigh it on a **certified digital scale** and send you a clear photo of the digital readout on **LINE (`@nonamelaundry`)** or **WhatsApp (`+66 80 888 9885`)** before billing.

### 2. What happens if my laundry bag weighs less than the 4.0 KG minimum?
Our minimum order weight for by-the-KG services is **4.0 KG** (which starts at just **฿280** for Standard Wash & Fold). If your bag weighs 3.2 KG, it is simply billed at the 4.0 KG minimum tier. To maximize value, most condo residents include a couple of bath towels, gym clothes, or pillowcases to easily reach 4.0 KG.

### 3. Are my clothes washed together with other customers' laundry?
**Never.** Every single customer order is washed and dried in a **dedicated, sanitized single-client drum**. We never mix your garments with anyone else’s laundry, and our commercial washers run automated hospital-grade drum disinfection cycles between loads.

### 4. What water temperature and detergents do you use?
By default, our **Wash & Fold** and **Wash & Iron** programs use a **fabric-safe 30°C–40°C warm wash** paired with hypoallergenic, color-safe premium liquid detergents and gentle pH-balanced fabric conditioners. If you prefer **scent-free / sensitive-skin detergent** or **cold-water wash only** for delicate activewear, simply toggle that preference during online booking.

### 5. Can I include bed sheets or duvets in my by-the-KG bag?
Everyday clothes, bath towels, hand towels, and pillowcases can go straight into your by-the-KG bag. For large flatwork and bulky bedding—such as **fitted/flat bed sheets (฿80/pc)**, **duvet covers (฿90/pc)**, **mattress toppers (฿180/pc)**, and **King/Queen duvets or comforters (฿220/pc)**—we charge transparent per-piece rates because they undergo dedicated commercial high-extraction wash cycles and thermal sanitization.
