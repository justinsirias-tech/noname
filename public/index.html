Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our digital laundry service.

How do I pay for my laundry order?

We operate on a 100% cashless system powered by a secure payment gateway. We accept Thai QR PromptPay, credit/debit cards, TrueMoney, and automated online invoices. You will receive an official digital payment link once your garments are weighed.

What is the minimum weight requirement?

The minimum order weight is 4.0 KG. If your bag weighs less than 4.0 KG, the minimum 4.0 KG base charge applies.
